[06-14-2026] I'm Back: A New Cadence, the Model Book, and What I Learned

Returning with a system, not just a post.

🧭 First, the honest part

The last Trade2Swing weekly went out on February 1, 2026. Then I went quiet for four months. Two reasons:

  • A round-trip in META, HOOD, and PLTR that gave back a year's worth of gains and made the screen unfriendly for a while.
  • Mishandling SNDK as a swing when the setup was screaming "position trade" — and missing what turned into a monster move.

I needed time away to take stock, travel, and decide whether I was going to keep doing this in public. I'm back, but I'm coming back differently: with infrastructure that makes the weekly survivable even on bad weeks, and with a commitment to publish the misses as honestly as the wins. The misses are the lessons. Most public trading writing only celebrates the wins. That's the part that doesn't help anyone — including the person writing it.

📚 The first Model Book entry is live

Trade2Swing.com now hosts a proper Model Book — a growing library of true market leaders, studied chart by chart. The first entry went up alongside this post:

Strength buys strength: a real cohort from a real FTD →

The April 8, 2026 follow-through day produced a cohort of fifteen names — semis, memory, AI infrastructure — that delivered roughly 200% average gains, with the best name running +467%. The entry walks through the six gates that selected them, three annotated charts (NASDAQ, SOX, and a SOXL trade I rode for years and then missed re-entering on after the FTD — a textbook lock-out rally), and the honest caveats that keep a Model Book from reading like a victory lap.

The SOXL story alone is the lesson I'd most want a new reader to absorb:

Selling well is half the job. Re-entering on a clean signal is the other half. After a confirmed FTD with leadership already at new highs, the move can run for weeks without giving sidelined money one clean pullback. That is the definition of a lock-out rally. When the signal arrives, the signal is the entry. There is no "wait for the dip" rule that helps here.

If you read one thing on the site, read that entry.

📊 Market read — week of June 8–12

Indices are in pullback mode, but semis continue to show leadership — no damage to the post-April FTD leaders. Net new high vs. new low is positive and elevated, which is the breadth tell that says the rally is still intact underneath the index noise.

The line to watch is the distribution-day count: S&P at 4, NASDAQ at 5. Five is the threshold where an uptrend moves from "confirmed" to "under pressure." One more distribution day on either index and the read changes.

Some healthcare names are showing strong leadership inside this pullback — new leadership emerging while the existing leaders hold is a healthy tape signal. Closely watching, and trading by the chart, not the news.

How these lists work

A working surface, not a tip sheet

These are the lists I work from each week. Names land here only after passing the gates that matter: a constructive base, leadership in a strong group, fundamental support, clean technical structure. They are a study surface — never a recommendation to act. The action plan for any single name belongs in the weekly spotlight, with pivot, stop, and sell rules written before any position is taken.

Using the lists

How to read these

A working definition of each list and what it means for action.

Ready

Names already at or pressing a clean pivot. The setup is intact, the trigger could come any session. These are the first names I'd act on if the broad market signal supports.

Watch

Leaders building constructive bases. Not yet actionable, but worth knowing the levels on. The discipline here is patience — let the chart come to you.

IPO

Recent IPOs forming early-stage bases or first-leg setups. Higher reward, higher risk. Position size accordingly when one of these graduates to Ready.

Click a ticker

Sourcing

Names earn a place through CANSLIM®-style screens, TradingView and TC2000 scans, group leadership analysis, and the weekly earnings dashboard. Process documented in the Trade2Swing approach.

Action discipline

A name being on Ready is not a buy signal. The buy signal is the chart action plus a market regime that allows it. Every trade carries a pre-written stop and sell plan.

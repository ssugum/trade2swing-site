Instrument D — Leadership & your own positions

The real-money tape — the most honest instrument

Indicators lag; the tape does not. The cleanest read on direction is how the true market leaders — and your own holdings — are actually behaving. When the best stocks and your best setups start acting badly, that is often the earliest and most honest warning you'll get, well before it shows up in the indices.

Healthy tapeDeteriorating tape
Breakouts work and hold their gains.Breakouts fail and reverse — bull traps.
Leaders trend and reset in orderly bases.Leaders break down on heavy volume.
Pullbacks get bought; dips stay shallow.Gains give back; big red days close on the lows.
Broad, high-quality leadership.Leadership narrows, or rotates to defensive, low-quality names.
Your positions act well; stops sit safe.Relative-strength lines roll over; your stops keep getting hit.
The personal signal

When your own book stops working, listen

A subtle but critical read: when your own well-chosen setups suddenly stop working and stops keep triggering, the market is telling you something the indexes may not show yet. A change of character — the small, tight, orderly gains of a healthy trend giving way to wide, sloppy, heavy-volume swings — is the tape turning. Let it override the optimism of any single indicator.

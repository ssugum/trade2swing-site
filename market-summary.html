This week · Aug 24–28, 2026

The index held, the internals didn't

The index held its ground last week — but the internals didn't. Breadth was fine until Friday, when 382 stocks fell 4% against just 84 that rose. New-high leadership thinned sharply (net new highs dropped from 34 to 8), and distribution is quietly building (4 days on the NASDAQ, 2 on the S&P). The long-term trend is still intact — the 50-day sits above the 200-day — and leadership rotated cleanly out of biotech into software. The net read: under pressure, not broken — selective, with fewer, smaller, tighter positions. The one number to watch is T2108 at 41.9; under 40 and this turns defensive.

The verdict
UNDER PRESSURE — but the window is still open
Posture
Selective. Fewer, smaller, tighter.

The method

How this read is built

Every reading above comes from the same four-instrument panel — and no single gauge decides it. You weigh them together, then let the balance set how aggressive to be.

A · Market breadth

Is money flowing in or out, and across how many stocks?

B · Distribution & follow-through

Are big institutions quietly selling — or has a bottom been confirmed?

C · New highs vs. new lows

Are more stocks hitting one-year highs, or one-year lows?

D · Leadership

Which stocks are actually being bought right now?
Green — confirmed uptrend: offense Amber — under pressure: caution Red — correction: defense
